2 · Rates and takes, per venue

what each venue charges before you are ahead

Kalshi's fee carries a P×(1−P) term, so it peaks at a coin flip and collapses at the tails. That single fact decides where this strategy can work: a 50¢ contract costs about 1.75¢ per contract to enter, a 5¢ or 95¢ contract about 0.33¢ — five times cheaper. The two columns point opposite ways because a hedge fixes your payout (so cost per contract is what bites) while a one-way bet fixes your stake.

5 · Championship fields

Add up every team's price to win the title. Under 100% and buying the whole field is a guaranteed payout for less than it returns. Over 100% is the overround, which is the exchange's edge over the room.
