DoodleLife: third-party software, models and data

DoodleNet doodle classifier (models/doodlenet/), by Yining Shi / ml5.js,
from https://github.com/ml5js/ml5-data-and-models. MIT License, full text below.
  DoodleNet was trained on the Quick, Draw! dataset, which Google, Inc. makes available
  under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The test fixtures in tests/fixtures/doodles.json
  are drawings from that dataset (https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset).

MediaPipe Tasks Vision (vendor/mediapipe/) with the BlazeFace short-range face detector, used only
when you turn the camera on: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
  Apache License 2.0, Copyright Google LLC. Full text: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Camera frames are processed on your device only and never uploaded. Your drawings stay in the page
unless you press Share, which packs them into the link itself (nothing is stored on a server).
