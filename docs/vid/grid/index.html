HOW MUCH PREJUDICE DOES IT TAKE
TO SPLIT A CITY IN TWO

A board of two colours, mixed at random. Every square wants two of its eight neighbours to match it -- and after it settles, the board is still mixed. Change the two to a three, with the same board and the same seed, and it sorts into continents. Nobody on the board wanted that: a square needing three of eight is content being outnumbered five to three. The simulation runs inside the video.
