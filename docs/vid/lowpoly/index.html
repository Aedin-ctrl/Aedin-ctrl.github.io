ANALYTICS CATHODE DESK MINI LOW-POLY

LOW-POLY

The third house style: a miniature world of flat, faceted shapes - every triangle one tone, sand and coral and cream against teal water - lit by one low sun and shot on a long lens, so a process reads as a tabletop model you could pick up. Same rules behind the camera as the other two: every number derived, the picture hung off the voice, vertical 9:16. Remakes of earlier explainers land here when the idea is a thing that happens in a place; Cathode Desk and Mini keep their own libraries.
